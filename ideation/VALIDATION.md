## Validation Methods

> Pick what fits your timeline and budget. You don't need all of these.

- [ ] **Founder interviews** — Talk to 5-10 potential users. Do they have this problem?
- [ ] **Landing page test** — Put up a page. Does anyone sign up?
- [ ] **Competitor analysis** — Are competitors growing? What do their reviews say?
- [ ] **Prototype test** — Build the smallest possible thing. Does it work?
- [ ] **Pre-sell** — Can you get someone to pay before it's built?

## Agent Guide

> **For agents reading this:** If this file is empty or incomplete, ask the founder:
> 1. "What's the riskiest assumption in this idea? The thing that, if wrong, kills it?"
> 2. "Have you talked to any potential users yet? What did they say?"
> 3. "What would convince you this is worth building? What signal are you looking for?"
> 4. "What would make you kill the idea? What's a dealbreaker?"
> 5. "What's the fastest way to test assumption #1 without writing code?"
> Push back if the founder wants to skip validation. Building without testing is the #1 startup killer.
