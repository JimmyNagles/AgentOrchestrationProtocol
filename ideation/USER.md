## Agent Guide

> **For agents reading this:** If this file is empty or incomplete, ask the founder:
> 1. "Describe your ideal user. Who's the first person that would use this?"
> 2. "What does their typical day look like around this problem?"
> 3. "What tools do they already use? How technical are they?"
> 4. "What would make them try something new? What's the switching cost?"
> 5. "Who should we NOT build for right now?"
> Do NOT move forward until you have a clear picture of ONE specific user.
